OUR STORY

“Rooted in tradition, served with love.”

SSunnyFounder & Head Cook

Sunny's Kitchen started at a family table in Antigua: big pots of pepperpot on Saturdays, ducana steaming in banana leaves, and nobody leaving hungry. Today we cook those same recipes in small batches each week, so every order tastes like it came from home.
